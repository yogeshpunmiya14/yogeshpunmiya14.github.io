About

I'm a Data Scientist and AI Engineer with about six years of enterprise experience turning messy data into pipelines, dashboards, and decisions. I started out as a Data Analyst in Mumbai, moved into BI development, and have spent the last few years building Qlik and Power BI platforms on Azure for global teams.

I'm currently based in Jersey City, NJ, and completed my Master's in Data Science at Pace University's Seidenberg School. My focus these days is on agentic AI systems, RAG pipelines, and LangGraph based orchestration, alongside a long running research interest in deepfake voice defense for banking authentication.

Outside of work, I play chess on Chess.com and used to compete in football tournaments during my undergrad years.
